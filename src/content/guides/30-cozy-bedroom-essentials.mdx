## Designing a Sanctuary of Calm

Your bedroom should be the one room in your home where the outside world dissolves. In Scandinavian interior styling, we call this concept **hygge**—a quality of coziness and comfortable conviviality that engenders a feeling of contentment.

### 3 Rules for Serene Bedroom Styling

1. **Eliminate Overhead Glare:** Turn off ceiling downlights after 8 PM. Rely exclusively on low-height bedside lamps and warm wall sconces.
2. **Embrace Tactile Contrast:** Pair smooth stoneware accessories with nubby boucle throws and washed linen pillowcases.
3. **Keep Surfaces Clear:** Dedicate your nightstand to just three items: a lamp, a carafe of water, and your current book.
